Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common technical queries regarding lifting tackles, material handling equipment, and safe industrial load movement.

Lifting tackles are mechanical devices and equipment used for lifting, pulling, positioning, tensioning, and handling heavy materials across industrial and commercial applications. They ensure controlled load movement and reduce manual labor while maintaining high safety standards across manufacturing, warehousing, and construction environments.

L.R. Engineering Works offers a broad range of lifting and material handling gear, including Wire Rope Pulleys, Cast Iron (CI) Wheels, Hand Pallet Trucks, Beam Lifting Clamps, Crab Winches, Electric Winches, and complete industrial lifting tackle assemblies tailored to diverse load handling requirements.

Lifting tackles are widely utilized across manufacturing plants, warehouses, storage facilities, construction and infrastructure sites, engineering workshops, shipyards, and freight loading and unloading operations where heavy loads require safe, systematic, and controlled movement.

Proper equipment selection depends on total load weight, center of gravity, frequency of lifting, available headroom, environmental conditions (such as indoor, outdoor, moisture, or chemical exposure), and whether manual mechanical advantage or powered electric operation is best suited for the workflow. Always ensure that the equipment rating meets or exceeds the required working load limit.

A hand pallet truck is a hydraulic floor-level material handling device specifically designed for shifting palletized loads, containers, and heavy materials across warehouse aisles, factory floors, and loading docks with smooth maneuverability and minimal operator effort.

A beam lifting clamp provides a secure, temporary or semi-permanent rigging suspension point onto structural steel I-beams and H-beams. It allows hoists, pulleys, winches, and tackle blocks to be safely attached and suspended without welding, drilling, or causing structural modification to the beam.

Manual lifting equipment (such as crab winches and manual pulley blocks) operates via mechanical gearing and hand power, offering high portability and independence from electrical supply. Electric lifting equipment (such as electric winches) utilizes motor-driven power for rapid, effortless, and continuous load movement, significantly increasing productivity in high-volume industrial environments.
