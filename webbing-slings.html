Round Endless Sling

Glider round endless slings are constructed from a continuous loop of 100% polyester load-bearing fibers. These core fibers are enclosed in a seamless, heavy-duty woven polyester protective sleeve. The load-bearing core never touches the load directly, preventing cuts, abrasions, and UV degradation.

Technical Datasheet
Product Code LREW-WS-02
Material 100% Polyester
Standard EN 1492-2
Safety Factor 7:1
Elongation Less than 3% at WLL
Key Features
  • Continuous endless loop design
  • Seamless polyester jacket protects core fibers
  • Even wear distribution by rotating load points
  • Low elongation and high load capacity
Applications
  • Rigging irregular-shaped loads
  • Heavy industrial lifts
  • Choker or basket hitches
