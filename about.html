About L.R. Engineering Works

L.R. Engineering Works, proudly operating under the brand LRLIFT, is a professionally managed engineering company specializing in the manufacturing and supply of high-quality lifting, rigging and material-handling equipment for industrial applications.

Established in 2017, the company has built its reputation through technical expertise, consistent quality, reliable service and customer satisfaction.
